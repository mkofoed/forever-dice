How it works

Click a race, class or body type to exclude it. Click Alliance or Horde to toggle the whole faction.

Choose the odds at the top: Every combo gives each valid race and class combination the same chance, so races and classes with many options come up more often. Every race picks a race first, then one of its classes. Every class picks a class first, then a race that can play it. Skyborne comes in two versions, High Order (Alliance) and Windshaper (Horde), each with its own classes.

Your exclusions are remembered in your own browser.

Combinations follow Wowhead's race and class combinations guide. See also Skyborne overview and racial traits. Fan-made, not affiliated with Blizzard.
